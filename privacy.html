Privacy policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

whiteboard-yt is an internal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel CHANNEL_NAME. It has no other users.

Data accessed through Google / YouTube APIs

  • The tool uses the YouTube Data API only on the owner's own channel, to upload videos, set their thumbnails and upload their captions.
  • It does not read, collect or store data about other users, viewers or channels.
  • The OAuth token is stored encrypted on the owner's own server and is used only for the uploads above.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The tool's use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
