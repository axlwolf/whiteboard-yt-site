whiteboard-yt

Internal tool · Herramienta interna

What it is

whiteboard-yt is a private, self-hosted tool used by a single person — the owner of the YouTube channel Axel's Whiteboard — to produce whiteboard-style explainer videos for that channel.

The owner writes a topic and a brief, reviews and approves every script, and the tool prepares the video, thumbnail and captions on the owner's own computers. The YouTube Data API is used only to upload those videos to the owner's own channel, as private or unlisted; the owner publishes them manually from YouTube Studio.

The tool is not offered to other users and does not access, store or display data from other users or channels.

Qué es

Herramienta privada y autoalojada que usa una sola persona, la dueña del canal, para producir videos explicativos tipo pizarra para su propio canal. Cada guion se revisa y aprueba a mano antes de subirse.
